Language Tutor — Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 17, 2026

Language Tutor is designed so that your data never leaves your device. In short: we do not collect, store, share, or sell any personal information — from anyone.

Data collection

Language Tutor collects no data. The app has no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party software development kits. It makes no network connections and works fully offline.

Microphone and speech recognition

If you choose to practice speaking, the app asks for permission to use the microphone and speech recognition. Audio is transcribed entirely on your device using Apple's on-device speech recognition. Your voice is never recorded, stored, or transmitted anywhere. You can revoke these permissions at any time in the Settings app.

Data stored on your device

Your learning progress and preferences (such as the selected language and theme) are saved only in the app's local storage on your device. This information is never transmitted, and it is deleted when you delete the app.

Children's privacy

Because the app collects no data at all, it collects no data from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a new effective date.

Contact

If you have any questions about this policy, email app.language.tutor@gmail.com.
